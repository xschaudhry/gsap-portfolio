Behind the demo

How the workflow works

  1. Capture. A website form, missed-call text-back or ad lead form sends the inquiry with the customer's consent to text.
  2. Sort by urgency. Simple rules read the message. No heat or no cooling goes to Urgent repair, replacement and quote requests go to Estimate, and the rest go to Standard service.
  3. Reply within a minute. The first text names the problem, offers three open windows and says how to opt out. An urgent inquiry also creates a staff call task for the on-call dispatcher right away.
  4. Respect quiet hours. From 9 PM to 8 AM, texts to non-urgent leads are held until 8 AM. Urgent call tasks and texts still go out right away.
  5. Adapt to the reply. Picking a number books the window. A price question gets “our office will confirm pricing” plus the times again. CALL creates a callback task. “Not interested” or STOP closes the lead.
  6. Follow up, then close. Each track has its own timing. A silent urgent lead gets one follow-up text while its call task stays open. Standard and estimate leads close after a last check-in.

What a real business would need to connect

  • Lead sources that post new inquiries by webhook: website forms, call tracking, ad lead forms.
  • An SMS provider with a registered business number (A2P 10DLC in the US), opt-in records, STOP handling and quiet hours.
  • Real availability from field service software or a dispatch calendar, so offered windows can actually be booked.
  • A CRM or pipeline that stores each lead, its status and the full message history.
  • On-call routing for urgent alerts and callback tasks, including after hours.
  • Owner-approved content: message wording, service area, quiet hours, and who confirms pricing.
  • Approved reply rules. This demo uses scripted keyword rules, not AI.

What this demo does not do

  • No texts are sent and no calls are placed.
  • No network requests. Everything runs in this tab and resets on reload.
  • No real calendar, client or results. Times and people are samples, and no prices are quoted.

HVAC Speed-to-Lead is a portfolio prototype by Chaudhry Usman. Copperline Heating & Air, its customers, addresses and phone numbers (555-010 range) are fictional. Every text is simulated.
